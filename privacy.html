LeonAI Upload

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

LeonAI Upload is a private publishing tool operated by Abdul Kader Sabouni (contact: jipoentertainment@gmail.com). It publishes short explainer videos produced by the operator to the operator's own social media accounts (channel “LeonAI Explains”). It is not offered to the public; the only user is the operator.

2. What data the tool processes

  • Access tokens that YouTube (Google), TikTok and Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) issue when the operator connects their own account via OAuth.
  • Basic account information returned by these platforms: account/channel ID, username or channel name, page ID.
  • Information about the operator's own videos: title, description, upload status, video ID/URL and public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).

The tool does not collect data about other people. It does not read, store or process viewers' personal data, comments' authors, messages or contacts.

3. Why

Solely to upload the operator's own videos to the operator's own accounts and to show the operator how those videos perform. No advertising, profiling, sale or sharing of data.

4. Where data is stored and who receives it

All data stays on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator's user account. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (e.g. a video to YouTube via the YouTube API). No other third parties receive data.

5. YouTube API Services

LeonAI Upload uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Statistics retrieved from the YouTube API are refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

6. TikTok and Meta

TikTok data is handled under the TikTok Privacy Policy; access can be revoked in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Meta data is handled under the Meta Privacy Policy; access can be revoked at Facebook → Business integrations.

7. Retention and data deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or the account is disconnected; then they are deleted from the operator's computer. Video information is kept as long as the video is online. To request deletion of any data, write to jipoentertainment@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. The request is completed within 30 days and confirmed by email.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data and object to processing, and you may complain to a data protection supervisory authority.
